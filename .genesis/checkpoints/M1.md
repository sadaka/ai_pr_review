## Quiz-me gate · 2026-08-22
1. (design decision) Why DiskANN over HNSW/ivfflat for code_chunks.embedding?
   - Answer given: "HNSW isn't available in Postgres at all" — INCORRECT. pgvector has supported
     HNSW natively since v0.5. Correct reason: DiskANN keeps most of the index on disk/SSD rather
     than requiring the full search graph in RAM, keeping Postgres realistic for large-scale vector
     memory as code_chunks grows into the millions, instead of an unbounded RAM cost.
2. (edge case) What happens without timescaledb_toolkit pre-installed?
   - Answer given: "It would fail — percentile_agg/approx_percentile undefined" — CORRECT.
3. (change impact) What does add_continuous_aggregate_policy enable?
   - Answer given: "A background job keeps the rollup auto-refreshed on a schedule" — CORRECT.
- 2/3 correct. Not a "don't know/skip" case, so verdict stays APPROVE, but the DiskANN rationale
  is flagged here as a real gap in understanding, corrected above for the record.
